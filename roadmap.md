The roadmap should be outcome-driven
I would avoid committing to the original Months 1–6 schedule before estimating integration effort and completing security and architecture reviews.

Instead, define phases with measurable exit criteria.
Phase	Main objective	Evidence required to proceed
Phase 1 — Foundation & Pilot	Establish approved hosting, connect Silobreaker and internal LLM, implement controlled ingestion and deliver an initial production workflow.	Successful end-to-end processing, access controls, auditability and demonstrated business value.
Phase 2 — Shared Intelligence & Reuse	Introduce reusable intelligence storage, onboard additional services and enable cross-service correlation.	Demonstrated reuse, measurable reduction in duplicated processing and validated read-across.
Phase 3 — Industrialisation & Scale	Expand service coverage, improve resilience, introduce wider dashboards and standardise operational processes.	Operational reliability, monitoring, recovery arrangements, stakeholder adoption and cost-performance evidence.

One important detail: the first pilot should be selected based on feasibility and measurable business benefit, not simply because a module is already live.

Daily Threat Intelligence is a reasonable candidate, but it should be assessed alongside Adverse News and UK Bank Risk Monitoring.
