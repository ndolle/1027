### Microsoft 365 – Major Service Disruption Across Multiple Regions

12 November 2026 | Incident EX1550543

On 12 November, Microsoft confirmed a major disruption affecting Exchange Online and Outlook, initially concentrated in North and South America, with significant user reports from the United States, including New York, New Jersey, Philadelphia and Houston, as well as Brazil. Microsoft subsequently warned that connectivity problems could affect users in any geographic region, including Europe, Asia-Pacific and the Middle East, although widespread disruption in each of these regions has not been independently confirmed.&#x20;

Affected services: Microsoft confirmed difficulties accessing Exchange Online mailboxes through multiple connection methods, including Outlook desktop, Outlook on the web and mobile email connections. Users reported failures to access inboxes, send or receive emails and synchronise mailboxes. Separately, disruptions were reported across Microsoft Teams, Microsoft 365 Copilot, OneDrive and SharePoint Online. Microsoft also identified Defender XDR among potentially affected services, although the extent of its disruption remains unclear. Teams experienced a separately tracked incident in North America (TM1550488).&#x20;

[image](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=https://www.neowin.net\&sz=32)

Neowin+2



The disruption escalated around 17:00–18:30 UTC, with more than 130,000 Outlook outage reports recorded on Downdetector. Microsoft identified elevated infrastructure load across multiple regions as a likely contributor to connectivity errors. Its response included redistributing traffic away from affected infrastructure and restarting servers to restore service.&#x20;

[image](https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=https://apnews.com\&sz=32)

AP News+1



Current understanding: Recovery measures have been implemented, with some reports indicating service restoration. The definitive root cause has not been publicly established, and there is no confirmed evidence of a cyberattack.
