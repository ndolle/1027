**Poll: Welcome to 2030. What Could Possibly Go Wrong?**

**1. The Autonomous Machine Economy**  
A time is coming when AI agents will own businesses, negotiate loans and trade with each other. Your next biggest client might be a machine that doesn't even know you exist!

**2. The Collapse of Digital Reality**  
A time is coming when your CEO, their voice, their signature and even their entire digital history could be artificial. Good luck explaining that to Internal Audit!

**3. The Programmable Human**  
A time is coming when humans may have AI chips connected to their brains, enhancing intelligence and decision-making. But who gets the final say — you or your software update?

**4. The Invisible Financial Infrastructure**  
A time is coming when banks may depend on millions of invisible digital connections. One tiny failure could bring the financial system to its knees — and nobody knows where the plug is!
