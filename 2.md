**Poll: Which emerging technology could pose the greatest systemic risk to financial services over the next 10 years?**

**1. The Autonomous Machine Economy**  
A business world where everything, from company creation and financing to decision-making and execution, happens machine-to-machine, with humans increasingly out of the loop.

**2. The Collapse of Digital Reality**  
A world where AI-generated people, identities, events and evidence become indistinguishable from reality. Even your auditors might need auditing!

**3. The Rise of AI-Augmented Humans**  
A world where some people make decisions with AI chips connected to their brains while others rely on natural intelligence. What happens when both compete in the same financial system?

**4. The Invisible Financial Infrastructure**  
A world where banks depend on millions of interconnected digital services that nobody fully understands or controls. One fails, and everyone discovers they were connected!

**By 2035, which technological revolution would challenge you the most?**

**1. Your Entire Office, Inside Your Glasses!**  
Three virtual monitors, a floating keyboard and an AI assistant wherever you look. Your next office could be a park bench!

**2. Your New Boss Is an AI!**  
An AI manages your human team, assigns work, evaluates performance and decides promotions. Good luck negotiating your salary increase!

**3. The Boardroom Without Humans!**  
An entire board of AI directors debates strategy, approves investments and oversees the CEO. Who takes responsibility when things go wrong?

**4. Your Digital Twin Goes to Work!**  
An AI version of you attends meetings, negotiates with colleagues and makes routine decisions while you enjoy your coffee. Until it promises something you never agreed to!
