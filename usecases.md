### Important distinction from EASM

I recommend keeping this as a separate intelligence service, but leveraging the same underlying technical capabilities.

| EASM                                                                                    | External Attack Surface Risk Monitoring                                                                    |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Focuses on discovering, attributing and assessing internet-facing assets and exposures. | Focuses on providing entity and métier-level visibility of external cyber risk.                            |
| Primarily supports technical exposure identification and asset inventory completeness.  | Primarily supports CISO oversight, risk assessment, benchmarking and management decisions.                 |
| Answers: What assets and exposures exist?                                               | Answers: What external cyber risks is my entity or métier exposed to, and how do they compare with others? |

Additional capability worth considering: Allow each CISO to select their entity or métier and access a consolidated risk dashboard showing exposure trends, critical vulnerabilities, unresolved findings, risk indicators and comparisons with other entities where authorised.

This would turn EASM intelligence into a self-service cyber risk oversight capability, rather than simply another technical monitoring tool.

I would therefore position this as Octopus's 12th intelligence service, with EASM providing a significant part of its underlying intelligence.
