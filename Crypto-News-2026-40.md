2. **CFTC Seeks to Define Prediction Event Contracts as Swaps Amid State Lawsuits**
   * The Commodity Futures Trading Commission initiated formal rulemaking to classify commercial prediction event contracts as swaps under the Commodity Exchange Act (CEA).
   * The rule establishes exclusive federal swap clearing oversight, pre-empting state-level gambling claims against prediction platforms like Kalshi and Polymarket.
   * This administrative action solidifies federal derivatives supremacy over event contracts, establishing clear regulatory boundaries for institutional prediction markets.
   * [Read more](https://cointelegraph.com/news/cftc-seeks-to-define-event-contracts-as-swaps-amid-prediction-market-fight)

3. **Circle Urges European Commission to Revise MiCA Stablecoin Reserve Deposit Rules**
   * Stablecoin issuer Circle joined European banking groups in formally petitioning the European Commission to scrap MiCA's 30%–60% commercial bank deposit mandate for stablecoin reserve backing.
   * The proposal recommends replacing commercial deposit requirements with 100% short-term sovereign paper or direct central bank reserve access to eliminate bank-run contagion risks.
   * This adjustment aligns European stablecoin reserve rules with US GENIUS Act standards, protecting commercial bank balance sheets while supporting compliant issuer expansion.
   * [Read more](https://cryptobriefing.com/circle-urges-eu-revise-mica-stablecoin-reserves/)

   * The authorization allows Coinbase to clear margin futures, options, and perpetual contracts natively for both institutional and retail market participants.
   * This approval creates a vertically integrated onshore derivatives venue, directly challenging offshore exchanges and legacy futures clearing merchants (FCMs).
