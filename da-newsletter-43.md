###### **Regulatory Update**

1. **OCC Relaxes Reserve and Yield Rules in Final GENIUS Act Regulations**
   * The U.S. Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) and Department of the Treasury finalized payment stablecoin regulations under the GENIUS Act.
   * The finalized rules relax previous reserve restrictions, allowing licensed payment stablecoin issuers to earn yield on short-term US Treasury reserves and central bank deposits.
   * This regulatory shift establishes a highly profitable, sovereign-backed operating framework for bank-backed stablecoin issuers ahead of the 2027 enforcement deadline.
   * [Read more](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/occ-relaxes-reserve-and-yield-rules-in-final-genius-act-stablecoin-regulations/)

2. **SEC Adds Tokenized Securities and DLT Registries to 2027 Examination Priorities**
   * The SEC Division of Examinations officially designated digital asset transfer agents, tokenized securities registries, and custody mechanisms as primary examination priorities for 2027.
   * The Division will audit registered broker-dealers and transfer agents for compliance with newly proposed on-chain disclosure, asset segregation, and key-management rules.
   * This examination focus signals that federal regulators expect institutional market participants to transition from experimental DLT pilots to fully audited production systems.
   * [Read more](https://cointelegraph.com/news/sec-adds-tokenized-securities-to-2027-examination-priorities)

3. **Cardano Foundation Urges EU Lawmakers Against Expanding MiCA Rules to DeFi**
   * The Cardano Foundation submitted a formal policy letter to European Union lawmakers urging against expanding MiCA regulatory mandates to non-custodial staking and DeFi protocols.
   * The Foundation argued that imposing VASP licensing requirements on decentralized validation nodes and open-source smart contracts contradicts MiCA's original legislative scope.
   * This policy intervention represents a coordinated push by major Web3 organizations to prevent burdensome banking-style compliance from stifling European DeFi development.
   * [Read more](https://cointelegraph.com/news/cardano-foundation-urges-eu-against-expanding-mica-rules-to-defi-and-staking)

4. **CFTC Chair Calls for Uniform Federal Crypto Regulations Amid State Disparities**
   * CFTC Chairman Mike Selig delivered an address advocating for a unified federal regulatory framework for digital asset derivatives to eliminate state-level jurisdictional friction.
   * Selig highlighted that conflicting state gambling laws and fragmented money-transmitter licensing create severe compliance hurdles for multi-state event markets and trading venues.
   * The address reinforces the CFTC's aggressive push to assert exclusive federal swap preemption over state gaming commissions and regional regulators.
   * [Read more](https://cryptobriefing.com/cftc-chair-calls-for-uniform-us-crypto-regulations-amid-state-disparities/)

5. **Börse Stuttgart Unit Seturion Obtains EU DLT Trading and Settlement License**
   * Börse Stuttgart Group digital asset entity Seturion secured official regulatory authorization under the EU DLT Pilot Regime to operate a combined trading and settlement system.
   * The license permits Seturion to operate a regulated DLT Multilateral Trading Facility (MTF) and DLT Settlement System (SS) for tokenized bonds, stocks, and fund shares.
   * This authorization establishes one of Europe's first fully integrated, bank-grade secondary trading venues for natively tokenized financial instruments.
   * [Read more](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/boerse-stuttgarts-seturion-gets-dlt-trading-and-settlement-license-as-eu-cap-debate-heats-up/)

6. **Central Bank of Ireland Fines Coinbase €3.6 Million Over Fund Safeguarding Failures**

   * Regulators found that the platform failed to adequately segregate client funds into dedicated safeguarding accounts within mandated statutory timeframes.
